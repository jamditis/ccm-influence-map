CCM influence and interaction map

Collection coverage and scoring

The working network includes contacts with CRM interaction or Board activity and journalism or media contacts with at least two mutual staff ties. The default sector filter focuses on journalism, media and local news. The wider view includes public follows and records without collaboration evidence.

CRM interaction scores start at four per interaction and halve every 180 days. Board links, program memberships and distinct staff named in CRM help break ties. Connections and reciprocal follows count toward mutual ties. Degree centrality is the share of other records one can reach by a single graph edge.

Organization scores include activity from affiliated people. A staff name in a CRM record establishes a mention. “Check CRM” flags limited identity evidence or gaps in the organization directory.
